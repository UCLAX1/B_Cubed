# Physical to BCM GPIO Conversion for B_Cubed

## Enc 0 — REV-11-1271 on 70kg Lazy Susan Servo
| Signal      | Physical | BCM |
|-------------|----------|-----|
| Power       | 17       | 3.3V |
| Ground      | 25       | GND  |
| Encoder A (blue)   | 37       | 26  |
| Encoder B (yellow) | 31       | 6   |
| Absolute (white)   | 29       | 5   |
| Servo data  | 33       | 13  |

## Enc 1 — REV-11-1271 on 5.5kg Head Servo
| Signal      | Physical | BCM |
|-------------|----------|-----|
| Power       | 1        | 3.3V |
| Ground      | 39       | GND  |
| Encoder A (blue)   | 7        | 4   |  ← moved from physical 13 (BCM 27), that pin is dead
| Encoder B (yellow) | 15       | 22  |
| Absolute (white)   | 11       | 17  |
| Servo data  | 12       | 18  |

## Other

## Notes
- The physical-to-BCM conversions above were verified on the Raspberry Pi 4.
- Set each REV-11-1271 side switch to A for ABI and absolute PWM output.
- REV-11-1271 quadrature output is 8192 counts per revolution.
- A working ABS (white) line emits a continuous 975.6 Hz duty-cycle signal,
  including while the shaft is stationary.
- Sense HAT occupies pins: 3, 5, 16, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 24, 27, 28
- Pins 4 and 6 are power in (5V)
- Issue: Relative encoder (27, 22) on enc 1 reads 0 steps even though servo spins and ABS=17 works.
  Try swapping: A=22, B=27 using test_encoder_swap.py
